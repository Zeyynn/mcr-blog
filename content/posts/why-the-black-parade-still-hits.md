*The Black Parade* is a story about a dying man, but it never feels like a funeral. It feels like a **celebration of being remembered**.

## The structure

The album opens with a quiet piano on "The End." and ends on "Famous Last Words", a final plea to keep going. In between, the Patient revisits regret, family, war and fame.

## The sound

Big choruses, theatrical arrangements and Ray Toro's guitars give each track the weight of a stadium anthem. Few rock records of the 2000s are this ambitious.

## Why it lasts

Because it speaks to anyone who has felt like an outsider: you are not alone, and you matter.
